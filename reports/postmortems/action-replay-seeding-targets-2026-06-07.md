# Action Replay Seeding Target Board - 2026-06-07

Internal helper-agent research. Not for publication. No scraping, fake engagement, or asset generation.

## Method

Public-source pass across indexed Instagram-adjacent pages, creator databases, public link pages, and visible creator writeups. Instagram pages were not directly fetchable in this environment, so follower counts are public-source estimates and should be verified in-app before shipping product. Story frequency and DM responsiveness are marked as inferred unless a source explicitly confirms posting/collab behavior.

Primary sources used:

- Retro gaming lists: Socialveins and Feedspot.
- Gyaru/J-fashion lists: Feedspot.
- Streetwear/fashion lists: Hafi, Influize, SocialBlade, Socialveins.
- Cultural-fit writeups: Nylon, Vogue, Dazed, NetInfluencer, Pictame.

## Read This First

Best immediate seeding lane: handheld/Pokemon/retro collector micro-creators plus gyaru/J-fashion micro-creators. They are much more likely to understand the object and repost the package without it feeling like an ad.

Best paid/repost lane: streetwear pages with high listed ER, but DM first and ask for transparent placement terms. Do not send product to pages that will not wear it.

Highest-risk lane: large fashion theorists and established digital fashion people. Huge cultural upside, but do not expect free organic posting.

## Missing Manual Verification Before Shipping

- Open each IG account in-app and verify current follower count.
- Check last 12 posts for comments that look real.
- Check story activity and highlight/package repost behavior.
- Check whether they already wear emerging brands.
- Check if account has product placement fatigue.
- Save 2 exact posts per target that prove the fit.
- Do not ship expensive packages until address and consent are confirmed.
