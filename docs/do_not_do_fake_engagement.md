# Do Not Do File

Internal helper-agent file. Keep this short enough to check before any growth experiment.

Do not use:

- fake followers,
- fake views,
- fake saves,
- fake comments,
- fake shares,
- engagement pods,
- comment spam,
- follow/unfollow loops,
- mass DMs,
- scraping restricted platform data,
- ban evasion,
- deceptive claims,
- stolen third-party creative assets,
- fake customer proof,
- fake receipts or fake shipping documents,
- side accounts that impersonate real third parties.

Allowed substitutes:

- official ads,
- creator reposts with permission,
- paid page placements with clear terms,
- manual community comments,
- manual data entry from exports/screenshots,
- product reality posts,
- customer proof only when real,
- weird fictional archive accounts that are ethically legible.

If a tactic would make the account feel bigger than it is by faking human behavior, reject it.
