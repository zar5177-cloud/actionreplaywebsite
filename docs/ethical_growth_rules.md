# Ethical Growth Rules

Internal helper-agent file. This is the risk boundary for growth work.

## Operating Line

Action Replay can be mysterious, fictional, and artifact-driven. It cannot rely on fake engagement, deception, scraping, stolen creative, or platform evasion.

The fiction must be legible enough that it does not become fraud. The product path must be especially clear.

## Allowed

- Organic posting.
- Official paid ads.
- Manual creator outreach.
- Paid reposts or sponsorships with clear terms.
- Creator whitelisting with permission.
- Public-source market research.
- Manual logging from platform exports and screenshots.
- Fictional side accounts that are ethically legible as archive/world accounts.
- Hidden codes and unlocks that do not require spam behavior.

## Not Allowed

- Fake followers.
- Fake saves.
- Fake shares.
- Fake comments.
- Engagement pods.
- Follow/unfollow automation.
- Automated commenting.
- Mass unsolicited DMs.
- Scraping restricted data.
- Platform ban evasion.
- Deceptive real-world claims.
- Stolen third-party assets.
- Fake reviews, fake sales, fake customers, fake partners.
- Fake receipts, legal notices, support messages, shipping notices, or transaction documents.

## SMM Panel Rule

Treat SMM panels as unsafe by default.

A service can be considered only if it is actually one of these:

- official ads,
- creator/influencer repost,
- newsletter placement,
- community sponsorship,
- transparent page placement with known audience quality.

If the service sells "real followers," "real saves," "real comments," "guaranteed viral reach," or account-network traffic, reject it.

## Unlock Mechanics Rule

Good:

- hidden code in a post edge,
- QR on packaging,
- story clue,
- waitlist framed as event flag,
- secret page,
- humane unlock window.

Bad:

- tag 3 friends for access,
- comment a keyword repeatedly,
- DM automation,
- fake scarcity,
- fake countdowns,
- pretending fictional archive accounts are real third-party institutions.

## Commerce Trust Rule

Lore can be weird. Buying cannot be weird.

Plainly state:

- what is for sale,
- quantity if known,
- shipping status,
- return/support path,
- whether a restock is exact or materially different.

Collector trust is part of the world.
