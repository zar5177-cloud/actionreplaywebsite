# Paid Ads Playbook

Internal helper-agent file. Paid media should amplify proven signals, not cover weak creative.

## Default Rule

Never spend big before organic proof.

Default test:

- objective: profile visits or engagement,
- budget: $8-12 per day,
- duration: 4 days minimum if the platform requires it,
- targeting: broad or Advantage-style unless the signal clearly supports a narrow audience,
- creative: organic winner carousel or reel.

Do not recommend $20/day for 5 days unless previous smaller boosts produced strong follow/profile quality, the website funnel is clean, the pixel or CAPI is verified, and a retargeting audience exists.

## Boost Eligibility

Boost if:

- non-follower reach is high,
- saves, sends, or shares are unusually strong,
- the post communicates the world without needing a lecture,
- product or account purpose is visible enough,
- profile is ready to receive traffic,
- follow-up posts support the same world.

Do not boost if:

- the post only looks cool,
- product is unclear,
- profile feels thin,
- comments show confusion that should be fixed first,
- engagement is shallow,
- budget would hurt operations,
- the creative depends on stolen assets or deceptive claims.

## Paid Test Hierarchy

1. Organic winner carousel.
2. Organic winner reel.
3. Creator-style product reality or wearer proof.
4. Warm audience retargeting.
5. Conversion campaign after funnel depth and event quality are proven.

## SMM And Reach Services

Allowed:

- official Meta/TikTok/Google ads,
- creator whitelisting with clear permission,
- paid influencer reposts,
- newsletter placements,
- paid community sponsorships,
- transparent meme or archive page placements with disclosed terms where needed.

Not allowed:

- fake followers,
- fake saves,
- fake comments,
- fake likes,
- fake account networks,
- engagement pods,
- bot views,
- automated comment tools,
- ban evasion,
- services that promise "real engagement" but do not disclose inventory source.

Any unclear service is treated as high risk until the operator can verify how reach is created.

## Test Log Fields

Every paid test must be logged in `data/ads.csv`:

- campaign id,
- dates,
- platform,
- objective,
- creative id,
- post id,
- spend,
- budget,
- views,
- reach,
- profile visits,
- follows,
- site clicks,
- add to cart,
- checkout,
- purchases,
- result note.

## Read The Result

Good early sign:
High profile visits, saves, sends, follows per reach, and comments that repeat a specific detail.

Weak sign:
Views and likes rise, but profile visits and follows stay flat.

Bad sign:
People ask what the product is, where the page is, or whether it is real in a way that blocks purchase trust.
