# Action Replay Growth System

Recovered 2026-05-20 from the operator folder that should not have been public.

This is not a normal clothing rollout. The brand should behave like a shop that was built on top of an old cheat-code archive, with product drops showing up as evidence instead of announcements. The growth loop is: make a believable artifact, let people wonder if it is real, give them a tiny action to investigate, reward the ones who notice, then archive the trace.

New layer: before anything goes out, run the human residue pass in `human-residue-layer.md`. The systems should not get bigger every time. They should get heavier: duplicate filenames, wrong timestamps, old warnings, dead experiments, recurring usernames, and small scars that imply someone cared too much before the audience arrived.

## Operating Rules

- No bots, fake engagement, follow/unfollow loops, mass DMs, scraping, purchased followers, engagement pods, or automated commenting.
- Side accounts must not impersonate real magazines, companies, communities, or people. They can be fictional archive accounts inside the Action Replay universe, but they need a quiet owner trail: bio hint, highlight, link page, or first pinned post that makes the fiction ethically legible.
- Comments should be manual, contextual, and low-volume. The line is “a strange person noticed something,” not “a brand is trying to juice reach.”
- Fake documents are artifacts, not fraud. Do not create real-looking bank receipts, invoices, shipping confirmations, legal notices, or support emails that could be mistaken for actual transactions with real third parties.
- The best posts should still work if the viewer never reads the lore.
- Never over-explain the world. Every post gets one unresolved detail, not a museum label.
- Do not optimize away drift. If a contradiction feels like old operational residue, keep it unless it creates legal, checkout, safety, or customer-support confusion.

Platform note: this system stays away from artificial likes/follows/shares, repetitive comments, repeated unsolicited commercial contact, scraping, and fake account deception because Instagram/Meta guidance explicitly treats those behaviors as spam or account-integrity risks. ## The Growth Engine

1. Artifact: a fake archive file, test print, receipt, forum capture, corrupted export, or hidden product clue.
2. Discovery: the post does not announce itself. It looks recovered, forwarded, scanned, screenshotted, or accidentally exported.
3. Participation: a code, timestamp, visual clue, folder name, comment prompt, hidden pixel, or password page gives people a reason to save/share.
4. Reward: secret page, discount, variant preview, waitlist flag, first-access email, sticker insert, or public unlock log.
5. Archive: the discovery becomes a file in the website archive, not disposable content.
6. Product: product is treated as physical evidence from the archive. The tee is not “new merch.” It is `AR-002 / SLOT A / recovered garment`.

## What To Build First

- Main account remains the cleanest, strongest signal. It posts the most finished artifacts, product reality, drop windows, and high-confidence discoveries.
- Side accounts are slower, weirder, and less polished. They create the feeling that Action Replay exists outside the main account.
- Website becomes the “save file.” Instagram creates the clue; the site stores the unlock.
- Email/SMS capture is framed as “watch this event flag,” not “join our newsletter.”
- Metrics track saves, shares, profile visits, unlocks, signups, and product interest more heavily than raw likes.

## Daily Manual Loop

At 9:00AM: check comments, save theory comments, reply to 5-12 strong ones, do not reply to every compliment.

At 12:00PM: publish main artifact or product-as-evidence post.

At 12:25PM: side account leaves one contextual comment or reposts to story if it has a reason.

At 3:00PM: publish secondary artifact, carousel detail, or story clue.

At 6:00PM: manually engage with 15-25 relevant community posts. No copy-paste comments.

At 9:00PM: post one looser story: desk photo, failed export, CRT photo, poll, code clue, or “found this folder again.”

End of day: log metrics in the dashboard schema. One sentence only: what got saved/shared and what felt too clean.

## Creative QA

Before posting, reject the asset if it feels like:

- a marketing campaign,
- a nostalgia moodboard,
- a perfect fake vintage ad,
- a joke with a punchline,
- a brand explaining its own lore,
- a clothing brand pretending to be weird for engagement.

Keep it if it has at least two of these:

- a timestamp that feels unnecessary,
- a cropped edge that implies a larger file,
- a mundane technical label,
- one emotionally wrong sentence,
- one unexplained code,
- a visible product clue,
- a reason someone might save it to zoom later.

Then add exactly one residue marker:

- `DEV-02 NEVER SHIPPED`
- `M-CARD REV E`
- `LAST VERIFIED: 2007`
- `DO NOT RUN ON SILVER MODEL`
- `TEMP LOCALIZATION FILE`
- `DEFECT PRESENT IN ALL COPIES`
- `wrong purple approved by nightstaff`
- `if duplicate appears, keep the older one`
